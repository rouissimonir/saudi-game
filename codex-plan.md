# Codex plan: Qahwa & Majlis

Last updated: 2026-11-01

## Goal and product identity

Build a leading Capacitor iOS merge game around Saudi hospitality: players prepare coffee and treats, welcome guests, personalize a majlis, and see their choices remembered. The owner selected Saudi Arabia's iPhone App Store Casual **top-grossing** chart as the first top-10 target. A ranking is an outcome to verify after release, not something source code or automated tests can establish. The measurement period and an independently verifiable source for the grossing chart still need to be chosen; record the source, device, territory, metric and observation time when checking it. Apple's Saudi Casual [free](https://apps.apple.com/sa/iphone/charts/7003?chart=top-free) and [paid](https://apps.apple.com/sa/iphone/charts/7003?chart=top-paid) views are separate charts and do not prove a grossing rank.

The source of truth for the design direction and pacing investigation is [docs/PRODUCT_DIRECTION.md](docs/PRODUCT_DIRECTION.md). Treat competitor rankings and other market claims in older documentation as historical and unverified until independently rechecked.

It recognizes the Apple refill purchase and confirmed refunds only for one unambiguous account identity, rejects unsupported quantities, production events and unhandled refund cases, limits request size, and sends no secrets or payloads to logs. Local classification and rejected-request tests pass; there is no deployed endpoint, configured webhook, live sandbox transaction or network-to-ledger test. A durable inbox, monitoring, transfer/refund-reversal reconciliation, account identity linking and in-game credit redemption remain necessary. RevenueCat's current documentation says webhooks depend on its Pro plan, so check the owner's actual plan and any cost before activation.

- **Hospitality and continuity:** Faisal's untimed coffee tasting lets players prepare trays, choose a coffee-corner style, and host Faisal and Noura. Faisal and Noura's order guides can also show their existing reaction to a restored wing and its current style, favoring the later matching wing. An optional first-restoration reveal can be revisited without awarding its reward twice. Existing painted scenes, wing artwork, mood fallbacks, and image prefetching were retained when the latest remote main was merged.

## Next priorities

1. **Native confidence.** The owner has Windows and an iPhone; use the [Windows-to-iPhone Safari preview](docs/WINDOWS_IPHONE_PREVIEW.md) for early touch and layout feedback. A native build still requires access to macOS and Xcode. On macOS, build and install the current iOS project in Xcode. Exercise fresh install, update from an older save, offline launch, interruption during a merge and a pour, background/resume, rotation, safe areas, small and large iPhones, VoiceOver, reduced motion, sound, and haptics. Confirm progress survives termination and relaunch; investigate any loss before release. Record device, iOS version, build commit, steps, and result.
2. **Observe the first gathering.** Use the [iPhone playtest protocol](docs/PLAYTEST_IPHONE.md) for early Safari feedback and moderated sessions with Arabic-speaking Saudi players on real devices, followed by native sessions when a signed build is available. Watch the first merge, first order, restoration, tasting and return visit without leading participants. Record hesitation, comprehension, emotional reactions, repeated actions, and whether they notice the guest memories and personalized details. Include Saudi cultural and dialect review of the Arabic, Taif references and visual details. The review packet ([docs/ARABIC_REVIEW.md](docs/ARABIC_REVIEW.md)) lists all 895 Arabic strings with their English meaning and location and the questions only a Saudi reviewer can answer; the review itself is not done.
3. **Make pacing decisions from evidence.** Measure actions and time to the first order, wing, gathering and later-area milestones, alongside voluntary return visits and energy waits. Compare with the simulator and player feedback before changing order requirements, energy costs or rewards. Check that the boost tradeoff is understandable and does not create an unfun stall.
4. **Improve the strongest identity moments.** Use playtest findings to deepen the welcome, the visible use of the player's majlis, and relationships that remember meaningful choices. Prototype improvements within the existing hospitality loop; validate discoverability and appeal before expanding content or adding new systems. Seven local hosts now open the area chapters and leave a keepsake each that the epilogue recalls (implemented; see [docs/CODEX_TASK_HOSTS.md](docs/CODEX_TASK_HOSTS.md) for what was verified). Not yet verified: whether players notice or value them, and the Saudi cultural and dialect review of their dress, names and Arabic lines.
5. **Prepare a responsible release.** Define the measurement period and source for the selected Saudi Arabia Casual top-grossing target, establish consent-aware analytics and baseline metrics, complete accessibility and native QA, and validate store assets and localization. A grossing target requires a viable revenue model; investigate offers players value without compromising the core hospitality loop, then implement and test real purchase and restore flows before enabling payments. [Apple permits up to two relevant Games subcategories](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/categories/); assess Puzzle as a second discovery category when configuring the listing. Keep fictional showcase entries clearly fictional. Avoid expanding timed tracks or social competition until the single-player loop earns repeat play and live operations can be supported.
6. **Evaluate the outcome.** After an appropriate release and sufficient player data, review retention, player feedback, technical stability, accessibility and independently sourced chart results for the agreed market and category. A top-10 claim requires matching, current evidence; this objective remains open until the evidence supports it.
