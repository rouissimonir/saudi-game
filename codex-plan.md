# Codex plan: Qahwa & Majlis

Last updated: 2026-10-28

## Goal and product identity

Build a leading Capacitor iOS merge game around Saudi hospitality: players prepare coffee and treats, welcome guests, personalize a majlis, and see their choices remembered. The owner selected Saudi Arabia's iPhone App Store Casual **top-grossing** chart as the first top-10 target. A ranking is an outcome to verify after release, not something source code or automated tests can establish. The measurement period and an independently verifiable source for the grossing chart still need to be chosen; record the source, device, territory, metric and observation time when checking it. Apple's Saudi Casual [free](https://apps.apple.com/sa/iphone/charts/7003?chart=top-free) and [paid](https://apps.apple.com/sa/iphone/charts/7003?chart=top-paid) views are separate charts and do not prove a grossing rank.

The source of truth for the design direction and pacing investigation is [docs/PRODUCT_DIRECTION.md](docs/PRODUCT_DIRECTION.md). Treat competitor rankings and other market claims in older documentation as historical and unverified until independently rechecked.

## Work completed so far

- **Hospitality and continuity:** Faisal's untimed coffee tasting lets players prepare trays, choose a coffee-corner style, and host Faisal and Noura. Serving a guest shows a non-blocking welcome with their portrait and reward after the daily gift closes. Their later guest orders can record a memory of the chosen tray, with the earned order reward displayed in the memory dialog. The corner and reactions can be revisited; after welcoming the guests, the scene is brought into view with its dialogue and optional celebratory feedback. The optional Taif rose welcome adds a regional hospitality choice and an enduring memory.
- **A personal majlis:** Renovated wings expose selected-style details and family reactions. When players travel after completing an area, the chosen wing styles are saved in a read-only “Places you restored” view with the area's artwork; tapping a saved wing shows its existing family reaction, and viewing it grants no second reward. Areas completed before this change cannot be reconstructed and are not invented. Faisal and Noura's order guides can also show their existing reaction to a restored wing and its current style, favoring the later matching wing. An optional first-restoration reveal can be revisited without awarding its reward twice. Existing painted scenes, wing artwork, mood fallbacks, and image prefetching were retained when the latest remote main was merged.
- **Clearer first session and goals:** A dismissible merge-and-serve coach precedes the daily login prompt. Guest cards show ingredients in a dedicated row; tapping a guest portrait opens the order guide, and ready orders retain a guest-specific Serve button. The repeated Guide buttons were removed following the owner’s iPhone feedback. Gathering and Taif recipe actions have larger touch targets and recipe-specific accessible names; selected-item Store and Sell controls have space when scrolled above the bottom navigation. Guest order and recipe guides show ingredient locations, next merge steps, boost-based direct generator options and their energy cost, rewards, pantry retrieval and optional board focus. The board shows the next gathering step from saved progress. A focused guest order resumes after reload and stops being active when served or cleared. A focused tasting recipe or Taif welcome also resumes, shows board ingredient progress, marks allocated ready ingredients with an explained board legend, identifies ingredients in the pantry and offers a ready-to-prepare action, and stops being active once prepared or cleared; preparing an unrelated tray leaves it intact.
- **Visible board guidance:** Tile shadows had obscured the outlines used for the first merge suggestion and other board highlights. Highlight borders now sit over the tiles, so the suggested pair, selection, matching targets and focused goals are visible while the tile shadow and keyboard focus remain available.
- **Accessible board guidance:** Board tiles now include their selected and matching-target state in their Arabic and English accessible labels, alongside the existing first-merge and focused-goal labels. Whether VoiceOver announces the changes at the right time still needs testing on an iPhone.
- **Portrait board visibility:** In response to the owner's iPhone 13 Pro feedback, the guest cards and board are placed together above secondary guidance in portrait mode, and the board scales to the available height. Guidance and the pantry remain available by scrolling. After the repeated Guide buttons were removed, the owner confirmed on their iPhone that the guests and full board remain visible together and that the layout looks good.
- **Board recovery:** Players can undo an eligible sale for a short period; the full-board prompt offers an order-aware spare merge when available and guidance otherwise. A full-pantry message now shows its actual occupancy and capacity, and other storage failures get separate guidance. A board-view control brings the grid into view. Empty and locked tiles allow vertical page scrolling, while movable items retain touch dragging. Reduced-motion mode suppresses merge flashes and movement while retaining a static reward label.
- **Owner feedback on controls:** Asked about the first session on iPhone, the owner selected merging and board controls as an area to improve, then clarified that the controls were okay and identified no specific difficulty. Observe actual play before changing their behavior.
- **Reliability and native packaging:** Save validation, including guest-order fields and rewards, backup recovery, transfer codes, storage-failure messaging, and wheel payout persistence have automated coverage. A failed save also offers an explicit retry and a transfer-code option. Editing a transfer code cancels any pending validation of the previous input; clipboard failure offers manual-copy guidance and transfer fields permit text selection. The Capacitor iOS project is present with haptics and a local web bundle; the bundle audit checks that built files match, flags unexpected leftover files, and checks that no remote server URL is configured.

## Next priorities

1. **Native confidence.** The owner has Windows and an iPhone; use the [Windows-to-iPhone Safari preview](docs/WINDOWS_IPHONE_PREVIEW.md) for early touch and layout feedback. A native build still requires access to macOS and Xcode. On macOS, build and install the current iOS project in Xcode. Exercise fresh install, update from an older save, offline launch, interruption during a merge and a pour, background/resume, rotation, safe areas, small and large iPhones, VoiceOver, reduced motion, sound, and haptics. Confirm progress survives termination and relaunch; investigate any loss before release. Record device, iOS version, build commit, steps, and result.
2. **Observe the first gathering.** Use the [iPhone playtest protocol](docs/PLAYTEST_IPHONE.md) for early Safari feedback and moderated sessions with Arabic-speaking Saudi players on real devices, followed by native sessions when a signed build is available. Watch the first merge, first order, restoration, tasting and return visit without leading participants. Record hesitation, comprehension, emotional reactions, repeated actions, and whether they notice the guest memories and personalized details. Include Saudi cultural and dialect review of the Arabic, Taif references and visual details. The review packet ([docs/ARABIC_REVIEW.md](docs/ARABIC_REVIEW.md)) lists all 895 Arabic strings with their English meaning and location and the questions only a Saudi reviewer can answer; the review itself is not done.
3. **Make pacing decisions from evidence.** Measure actions and time to the first order, wing, gathering and later-area milestones, alongside voluntary return visits and energy waits. Compare with the simulator and player feedback before changing order requirements, energy costs or rewards. Check that the boost tradeoff is understandable and does not create an unfun stall.
4. **Improve the strongest identity moments.** Use playtest findings to deepen the welcome, the visible use of the player's majlis, and relationships that remember meaningful choices. Prototype improvements within the existing hospitality loop; validate discoverability and appeal before expanding content or adding new systems. Seven local hosts now open the area chapters and leave a keepsake each that the epilogue recalls (implemented; see [docs/CODEX_TASK_HOSTS.md](docs/CODEX_TASK_HOSTS.md) for what was verified). Not yet verified: whether players notice or value them, and the Saudi cultural and dialect review of their dress, names and Arabic lines.
5. **Prepare a responsible release.** Define the measurement period and source for the selected Saudi Arabia Casual top-grossing target, establish consent-aware analytics and baseline metrics, complete accessibility and native QA, and validate store assets and localization. A grossing target requires a viable revenue model; investigate offers players value without compromising the core hospitality loop, then implement and test real purchase and restore flows before enabling payments. [Apple permits up to two relevant Games subcategories](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/categories/); assess Puzzle as a second discovery category when configuring the listing. Keep fictional showcase entries clearly fictional. Avoid expanding timed tracks or social competition until the single-player loop earns repeat play and live operations can be supported.
6. **Evaluate the outcome.** After an appropriate release and sufficient player data, review retention, player feedback, technical stability, accessibility and independently sourced chart results for the agreed market and category. A top-10 claim requires matching, current evidence; this objective remains open until the evidence supports it.
