# Codex plan: Qahwa & Majlis

Last updated: 2026-10-27

## Goal and product identity

Build a leading Capacitor iOS merge game around Saudi hospitality: players prepare coffee and treats, welcome guests, personalize a majlis, and see their choices remembered. Aim for the top 10 in a clearly defined App Store category and market without sacrificing that identity. A ranking is an outcome to verify after release, not something source code or automated tests can establish. The exact chart, country, metric, and measurement period still need to be chosen.

The source of truth for the design direction and pacing investigation is [docs/PRODUCT_DIRECTION.md](docs/PRODUCT_DIRECTION.md). Treat competitor rankings and other market claims in older documentation as historical and unverified until independently rechecked.

## Work completed so far

- **Hospitality and continuity:** Faisal's untimed coffee tasting lets players prepare trays, choose a coffee-corner style, and host Faisal and Noura. Serving a guest shows a non-blocking welcome with their portrait and reward after the daily gift closes. Their later guest orders can record a memory of the chosen tray, with the earned order reward displayed in the memory dialog. The corner and reactions can be revisited; after welcoming the guests, the scene is brought into view with its dialogue and optional celebratory feedback. The optional Taif rose welcome adds a regional hospitality choice and an enduring memory.
- **A personal majlis:** Renovated wings expose selected-style details and family reactions. When players travel after completing an area, the chosen wing styles are saved in a read-only “Places you restored” view with the area's artwork; tapping a saved wing shows its existing family reaction, and viewing it grants no second reward. Areas completed before this change cannot be reconstructed and are not invented. Faisal and Noura's order guides can also show their existing reaction to a restored wing and its current style, favoring the later matching wing. An optional first-restoration reveal can be revisited without awarding its reward twice. Existing painted scenes, wing artwork, mood fallbacks, and image prefetching were retained when the latest remote main was merged.
- **Clearer first session and goals:** A dismissible merge-and-serve coach precedes the daily login prompt. Guest cards show ingredients in a dedicated row and use larger Serve and Guide touch targets with guest-specific Arabic and English accessibility labels. Gathering and Taif recipe actions have larger touch targets and recipe-specific accessible names; selected-item Store and Sell controls have space when scrolled above the bottom navigation. Guest order and recipe guides show ingredient locations, next merge steps, boost-based direct generator options and their energy cost, rewards, pantry retrieval and optional board focus. The board shows the next gathering step from saved progress. A focused guest order resumes after reload and stops being active when served or cleared. A focused tasting recipe or Taif welcome also resumes, shows board ingredient progress, marks allocated ready ingredients with an explained board legend, identifies ingredients in the pantry and offers a ready-to-prepare action, and stops being active once prepared or cleared; preparing an unrelated tray leaves it intact.
- **Portrait board visibility:** In response to the owner's iPhone 13 Pro feedback, the guest cards and board are placed together above secondary guidance in portrait mode, and the board scales to the available height. Guidance and the pantry remain available by scrolling. The owner has not yet tested this revision on the physical phone.
- **Board recovery:** Players can undo an eligible sale for a short period; the full-board prompt offers an order-aware spare merge when available and guidance otherwise. A full-pantry message now shows its actual occupancy and capacity, and other storage failures get separate guidance. A board-view control brings the grid into view. Empty and locked tiles allow vertical page scrolling, while movable items retain touch dragging. Reduced-motion mode suppresses merge flashes and movement while retaining a static reward label.
- **Reliability and native packaging:** Save validation, including guest-order fields and rewards, backup recovery, transfer codes, storage-failure messaging, and wheel payout persistence have automated coverage. A failed save also offers an explicit retry and a transfer-code option. Editing a transfer code cancels any pending validation of the previous input; clipboard failure offers manual-copy guidance and transfer fields permit text selection. The Capacitor iOS project is present with haptics and a local web bundle; the bundle audit checks that built files match, flags unexpected leftover files, and checks that no remote server URL is configured.
- **Cloud native build check:** A GitHub Actions workflow builds and audits a fresh web bundle, syncs Capacitor, and compiles the Xcode project for the iOS simulator without signing. It can be run manually and runs for changes to game source and assets, web build inputs, the native project, dependencies or workflow. On commit `5fc44c8`, the [simulator run](https://github.com/rouissimonir/saudi-game/actions/runs/40555719006) reported successful build, install, launch request and screenshot-upload steps. The screenshot has not been visually inspected; the check does not verify interactions or a physical iPhone.
- **Pacing research:** Deterministic economy simulations compare unboosted and adaptive-boost strategies. They highlight substantial later-area effort and energy waits, but are not estimates of human enjoyment or proof of appropriate balance. See the product-direction document for assumptions and results.

## Next priorities

1. **Native confidence.** The owner has Windows and an iPhone; use the [Windows-to-iPhone Safari preview](docs/WINDOWS_IPHONE_PREVIEW.md) for early touch and layout feedback. A native build still requires access to macOS and Xcode. On macOS, build and install the current iOS project in Xcode. Exercise fresh install, update from an older save, offline launch, interruption during a merge and a pour, background/resume, rotation, safe areas, small and large iPhones, VoiceOver, reduced motion, sound, and haptics. Confirm progress survives termination and relaunch; investigate any loss before release. Record device, iOS version, build commit, steps, and result.
2. **Observe the first gathering.** Use the [iPhone playtest protocol](docs/PLAYTEST_IPHONE.md) for early Safari feedback and moderated sessions with Arabic-speaking Saudi players on real devices, followed by native sessions when a signed build is available. Watch the first merge, first order, restoration, tasting and return visit without leading participants. Record hesitation, comprehension, emotional reactions, repeated actions, and whether they notice the guest memories and personalized details. Include Saudi cultural and dialect review of the Arabic, Taif references and visual details.
3. **Make pacing decisions from evidence.** Measure actions and time to the first order, wing, gathering and later-area milestones, alongside voluntary return visits and energy waits. Compare with the simulator and player feedback before changing order requirements, energy costs or rewards. Check that the boost tradeoff is understandable and does not create an unfun stall.
4. **Improve the strongest identity moments.** Use playtest findings to deepen the welcome, the visible use of the player's majlis, and relationships that remember meaningful choices. Prototype improvements within the existing hospitality loop; validate discoverability and appeal before expanding content or adding new systems. Seven local hosts for the area chapters are drafted but not wired in; the work for later is in [docs/CODEX_TASK_HOSTS.md](docs/CODEX_TASK_HOSTS.md), waiting on the portraits in `docs/ART_BATCH_8.md`.
5. **Prepare a responsible release.** Decide the target chart and market, establish consent-aware analytics and baseline metrics, complete accessibility and native QA, and validate store assets and localization. Keep fictional showcase entries clearly fictional. Do not enable real-money purchases until a real purchase and restore flow is implemented and verified. Avoid expanding timed tracks or social competition until the single-player loop earns repeat play and live operations can be supported.
6. **Evaluate the outcome.** After an appropriate release and sufficient player data, review retention, player feedback, technical stability, accessibility and independently sourced chart results for the agreed market and category. A top-10 claim requires matching, current evidence; this objective remains open until the evidence supports it.
