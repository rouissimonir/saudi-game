**Prompt:**

> Reference 1 is Layla, the heroine of a Saudi story game: match her face, emerald silk hijab with the thin gold band, cream abaya with gold trim and gold pendant exactly, and match the semi-realistic painted style (soft painterly shading, warm golden rim light from the left). Reference 2 is the painted Diriyah street at sunset from the same game: match its setting, colours and light.
>
> Paint a tall vertical 9:16 key art illustration for the game's title screen. Layla stands in the lower-middle of the frame, seen from the waist up and turned slightly to the left, smiling warmly and proudly, holding her grandfather's polished brass dallah (a traditional Arabic coffee pot with a tall crescent-shaped spout) in both hands in front of her. Behind her rises the restored family Majlis, a Najdi mud-brick building of warm ochre clay with stepped triangular crenellations, small triangular openings and a carved wooden door with geometric paint, glowing with warm lantern light in its windows. Date palms frame the sides, and a large low sun sets on the left in a gold, peach and rose sky. A few soft drifting rose petals and a gentle steam curl rise from the dallah's spout.

>
> Style: semi-realistic digital painting matching the references, believable proportions, warm and inviting, polished mobile-game key art. Not anime, not photoreal, not 3D. No text, letters, logos or watermarks.
