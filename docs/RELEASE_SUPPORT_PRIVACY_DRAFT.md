## Privacy policy

**Privacy Policy — Qahwa & Majlis Merge**  
**Contact:** mounirrouissi2@gmail.com  
**Last updated:** 12 November 2026 (change this to the day you publish)

### About this policy

This policy explains how Qahwa & Majlis Merge handles information when you play the iPhone game or contact us for support.

### Game progress

The game stores progress and settings on your device. If you choose to use the progress-transfer feature, the game creates a transfer code containing your game data; anyone with that code may be able to import it. Keep it private. Deleting the app may delete local progress. We do not provide an account-based cloud backup in this version.

### Purchases

This version of the game has no in-app purchases and no ads. We do not collect payment information.

### Information we do not collect

The game does not ask you to create an account, does not use analytics, advertising or tracking, and does not send your game progress to our servers. Nothing in the game identifies you to us.

### Support requests

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever information you choose to include. We use it to respond to your request and investigate the issue. Please do not send passwords or payment-card details.

### Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you remove it or delete the app. You can ask us to delete information you sent in a support email by contacting **mounirrouissi2@gmail.com**. We retain support correspondence only as long as reasonably needed to respond, resolve issues or comply with applicable obligations. 

### Your choices and contact

You can decide whether to contact support and what information to include. For privacy questions or a deletion request, email **mounirrouissi2@gmail.com**.

### Changes

We may update this policy as the game changes. The latest version will be available at this page, with the date above updated.
